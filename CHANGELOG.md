# Changelog

## v0.1.0 · 2026-10-15

First release: a live Lower Manhattan where about 600 travellers follow Overture's transport
rules, evaluated in the browser by [overrules](https://crates.io/crates/overrules) 0.1.

- **The city.** Cars, vans, lorries, bikes and pedestrians ask overrules about every way out of
  every junction. A live feed shows each refusal and who made it: overrules (the data) or a
  labelled game rule (where the data is silent).
- **The tour.** Eleven self-running scenes on real streets: last match wins, time windows, the
  travel-mode tree, "can't be sure", vehicle size, one- and two-step turn bans, repaired
  opening hours, geodesic vs flat positions, throughput, and live refusals. Scenes can be
  linked to (`#03`).
- **Explore.** Click any road for its rules in plain English and a what-if grid across
  vehicles, times and claimed purposes; click any traveller for its last decision.
- Styled after xerj.org, with day and night themes. Built from Overture release 2026-09-23.1.
